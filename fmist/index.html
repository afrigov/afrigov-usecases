How you know this is unofficial

This is a demonstration built on afrigov. It is not run by the Federal Ministry of Innovation, Science and Technology. The real website is scienceandtech.gov.ng.

Official websites use .gov.ng. This one does not, and it carries no government seal. Nothing you type here is sent to the ministry.

Services

The ministry's agencies deliver these services. Each link goes to the agency that handles it.

  • Patent registration

    Protect an invention, so you hold the rights to it.

    Handled by National Office for Technology Acquisition and Promotion (NOTAP)

  • Technology transfer registration

    Register an agreement to bring in or license a technology, between inventors, researchers and companies.

    Handled by National Office for Technology Acquisition and Promotion (NOTAP)

  • Technology incubation

    Space, mentoring and support for an early-stage technology business.

    Handled by National Board for Technology Incubation (NBTI)

Youth and Students in Innovation 2026

Practical training in digital skills, AI and innovation for Nigerians aged 18 to 30: three weeks online, then three days in person in Enugu.

  • Eight tracks, from software and AI to agritech and fintech
  • Mentoring after the programme
  • Applications closed on 14 September 2026
